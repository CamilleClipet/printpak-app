Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

printpak (the website and the iOS and Android apps) turns photos into black-and-white images for small thermal printers. It is designed to work without collecting any personal data.

Data we collect

None. printpak has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run any server that receives your data.

Photos and camera

Photos you import and images from the camera are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded. When you save an image or a video, it is saved to your device (for example your Photos library), only when you ask for it.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to find and connect to your thermal printer and to send it the image you print. On Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices; printpak does not access or use your location.

Settings stored on your device

Your settings, presets and the name of your last printer are stored locally on your device so they are kept between launches. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the website's data) deletes them.

Lyrics search

If you use the lyrics search, the song title or text you type is sent to LRCLIB, a free third-party lyrics service, to find matching lyrics. Like any website, LRCLIB receives your IP address with the request. printpak sends nothing else. This feature is optional.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

printpak does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
