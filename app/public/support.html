printpak support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support.keepitsimple.dev@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, your printer model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Compatible printers

printpak prints to small Bluetooth thermal "cat printers" of the X6 family (384 dots wide, 58 mm paper).

The printer is not found

  • Make sure the printer is switched on and charged, and close to the phone.
  • Allow Bluetooth for printpak in your phone's settings.
  • If the printer is connected to another phone, disconnect it there first.
  • Switch the printer off and on, then tap Search again.

Prints are too light or too dark

Change the darkness on the export screen, or print the calibration chart from Settings and follow the steps there.

Saving images

On the export screen, press and hold the image, then choose Save to Photos (on a computer, right-click it and choose Save image as…). To save a video, tap Save after recording.

Privacy

printpak collects no data. See the privacy policy.
